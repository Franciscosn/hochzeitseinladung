# K und F – reine Schrift

Mit dem integrierten Imagegen-Werkzeug erstellt. Ein K links und ein F rechts, dunkelrote kursive Schrift auf Elfenbein. Auswahlentwurf.

## Prompt

Create a minimalist typographic study containing EXACTLY TWO LETTERS: uppercase 'K' on the LEFT and uppercase 'F' on the RIGHT, reading 'K F'. Nothing else. Elegant restrained italic calligraphy, classic cursive letterforms but absolutely clear and readable. Each letter has one uncomplicated natural shape: K has a clear stem and two diagonal arms; F has a clear stem, top arm and shorter middle arm. Keep the two letters separate with comfortable space between them. NO interlacing, NO overlapping, NO swashes, NO extended flourishes, NO curls below letters, NO ribbon, NO extra strokes, NO ornaments, NO crown, NO plants, NO ampersand. Solid deep burgundy ink on plain uniform warm ivory background. Flat clean typography only, NOT a wax seal, no circular frame, no relief, no shadows, no texture, no mockup. Center the two letters as a balanced pair in a square image with generous margins. Only one K and one F.
