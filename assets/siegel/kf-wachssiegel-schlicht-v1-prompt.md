# Schlichtes K–F-Wachssiegel

Mit dem integrierten Imagegen-Werkzeug erstellt. K links, F rechts in kursiver Schrift auf dunkelrotem Wachs. Auswahlentwurf auf Elfenbeingrund.

## Prompt

Create a photorealistic wedding WAX SEAL. A complete circular freshly stamped DARK BURGUNDY RED wax seal, gently irregular poured outer rim, matte satin wax with very soft diffuse highlights, no glossy bright reflections. Inside the seal, ONLY EXACTLY TWO embossed letters: uppercase K on the LEFT and uppercase F on the RIGHT. Elegant SIMPLE italic calligraphy, very clearly readable, equal size, comfortably spaced apart. K has one stem and two clear diagonal arms. F has one stem, one upper arm and one shorter middle arm. Restrained short terminals only. Absolutely NO additional band, ribbon, interweaving, overlapping, extended swashes, loops, decorative strokes, ampersand, crown, wreath, branches, plants or other symbols. Just K and F in raised relief made from the same dark red wax. Thin simple circular inset border allowed. Beautiful fresh intact wax, no distressing, cracks or patina. Straight-on overhead view, centered complete seal fills 85 percent of square, warm plain ivory background, no paper props, no labels, no watermark, no checkerboard. The result must be a real tactile WAX SEAL bearing the two simple italic letters, not printed lettering on a page.
